Cetraro, Calabria

Practical Information

Participants normally arrive on the evening of 19 September. Departure may take place on 24 or 25 September.

The school will take place in Cetraro, a beautiful location on the Tyrrhenian coast of Calabria, Italy. Participants are lodged at the Grand Hotel S. Michele.

How to reach the conference venue

By train

The nearest station is Cetraro, on the Roma–Salerno–Reggio Calabria line, where local trains stop. Fast trains stop in Paola. Timetables are available from Trenitalia.

If informed in time, the hotel can arrange a car from Paola station. The service costs approximately €50–60 per journey, shared among passengers, and must be booked and paid directly with the hotel.

By plane

The nearest airport is Lamezia Terme.

If informed in time, the hotel can arrange a car from the airport. The service costs approximately €90–100 per journey, shared among passengers, and must be booked and paid directly with the hotel.

By car

From Rome, take the Roma–Napoli–Reggio Calabria motorway and exit at Lagonegro Nord, then follow the SS18 south along the coast; alternatively, exit at Spezzano Terme and follow the SS283 to Cetraro.
