{\large\bfseries\color{cimegold} Abstract}

In this course we will focus on one of the simplest examples of phase transition, namely percolation, which is the study of connectivity in random networks and graphs. We will introduce the models of bond and site percolation on lattices and trees and discuss important results regarding the sub and supercritical regimes.
