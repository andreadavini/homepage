CIME School · Cetraro, Italy

Hamilton–Jacobi Equations,
Games, and Percolation

Interfaces and Synergies

20–24 September 2027

This CIME School explores the emerging and fruitful connections between Hamilton–Jacobi equations, dynamic games, and probabilistic growth models, notably percolation. It will provide a shared language and methodological toolkit for researchers working across PDEs, Game Theory, and Probability.
